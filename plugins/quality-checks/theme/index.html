Questions people ask.

Where is my data stored?

In a Postgres database hosted on European servers, encrypted at rest, with backups every six hours. We hold the keys; you hold the data; if either of us goes away, you can leave with a single CSV.

What if I close my account?

You can export everything as CSV at any time. Closing the account deletes the data within 30 days, and we email a confirmation when the deletion runs. No dark-pattern retention, no “come back” sequences.

Do you sell anything to anyone?

No. There are no analytics SDKs, no ad pixels, no LLM training pipelines. We charge for Studio precisely so we don’t need to monetise the data.

Why does the free tier exist?

Because most freelancers don’t need multi-currency, and charging for the basics felt rude. Studio pays the bills; Free is the product the way it should work.
